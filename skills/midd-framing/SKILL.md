# Model-informed drug development framing

Define the decision, its timing and the stakeholders who will use the result. State how the analysis will change the decision and which findings would lead to each action. Specify the model context of use, target population and the consequences of a wrong conclusion.

Tie the analysis plan to declared assumptions, data provenance, uncertainty and the agreed risk level. Identify gaps that require sensitivity analyses or additional data. Identify which figures and tables support each decision and use findings from the reviewed artifacts in the final account.
