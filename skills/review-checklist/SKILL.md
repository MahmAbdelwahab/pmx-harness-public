# Analysis review

1. Verify the decision, context of use and acceptance criteria against the local analysis declaration.
2. Check dataset lineage, inclusion and exclusion rules, units and the reviewed manifest and gate records.
3. Reproduce the declared analysis in a disposable copy and compare artifacts. Record missing inputs and substantive discrepancies as review findings.
4. Inspect estimation, numerical diagnostics, plots and sensitivity analyses. Explain the implications for the stated decision.
5. Check the rendered report against its promoted artifacts and the scope of the local gate. Record unresolved issues and the reviewer disposition.
